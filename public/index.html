How We Manage Your Export Shipment

From contract specification alignment to bill of lading issuance, our dedicated export desk ensures clear communication, documentation compliance, and punctual maritime coordination.

Stage 01

Spec & Verification

We review your target purity, mesh size, and moisture tolerance, providing verified Certificates of Analysis (CoA) for technical compliance.

Stage 02

Sortex Processing

Lot procurement is passed through Buhler/Sortex optical colour sorters and destoners to eliminate impurities, dust, and immature seeds.

Stage 03

Custom Packing

Packed into 25kg/50kg PP bags, multi-wall Kraft paper sacks, or retail standup pouches with buyer private label branding and batch codes.

Stage 04

Port & Phyto Clearance

Full container stuffing at Nhava Sheva or Mundra ports, complete with Phytosanitary Certificate, Fumigation, and Certificate of Origin.
